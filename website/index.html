02 · what's new in 2.4.0

The biggest update yet.

  1. 01

    Livestreams on SABR

    YouTube's native streaming protocol, written in C#. Playback starts in about 2 s instead of 12, and long streams no longer stop after 40 minutes.

  2. 02

    Samples

    A vertical feed of music video clips from your Home and the local video charts.

  3. 03

    Word-by-word lyrics

    Karaoke animation, a new KuGou source, duet singers on either side and background vocals on their own line.

  4. 04

    Animated artwork

    Apple Music motion artwork on Now Playing, fading into the blurred backdrop.

  5. 05

    Multiple accounts

    Switch between YouTube accounts and brand channels.

  6. 06

    Listening stats & Last.fm

    Minutes, top songs and artists, your listening clock, and scrobbling even when the app is closed.

  7. 07

    New from your artists

    New albums and singles of the artists you follow, right on Home.

  8. 08

    Return YouTube Dislike

    Like, dislike and view counts in the Now Playing menu.

  9. 09

    DNS over HTTPS

    Cloudflare with a Google fallback, for networks that throttle YouTube.

05 · install

Ready in a few minutes.

From a store easiest

Install and update YTMusicWP from the Live Store, or get it from the WinArchive store.

Live Store WinArchive

Windows Phone 8.1

  1. Download the .appx, the .cer and Microsoft.VCLibs.ARM.12.00.Phone.appx from the latest release.
  2. Open the .cer on your phone (from email or a file manager) to install the certificate.
  3. Install VCLibs if the phone asks for it, then the .appx with WPAD, WPV Xap Deployer or Windows Phone Power Tools.

Windows 10 Mobile

  1. Turn on Settings › Update & security › For developers › Developer mode.
  2. Download the .appx to your phone, open it in File Explorer and tap Install.
